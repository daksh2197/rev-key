Where the revs come from

Plug in a USB wheel or gamepad and press the pedal: it drives this car.

An ELM327-type Bluetooth LE adapter in the car's OBD-II port, read only: engine speed, throttle and vehicle speed, several times a second. Nothing is ever written to the car. Set up parked.

Settings

What it is doing

  1. Firing note. A four-stroke engine fires cylinders ÷ 2 times per revolution. At 2,200 rpm a V6 makes 110 Hz: an A.
  2. Key lock. When the revs hold steady for a second, the song moves by the smallest shift (two semitones at most) that puts its root or fifth on that note.
  3. Lanes. Revs and throttle open the pump lane on the highway. Hard driving in town, or over the limit anywhere, closes it and lowers the volume.
  4. Shift drop. It learns where you shift and books the drop ahead of time, so it reaches the speaker on the gear change instead of after it.

The two beats are placeholders written for this demo, not released tracks. No account, no server, no tracking: everything runs on this page.
